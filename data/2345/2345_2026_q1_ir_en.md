1988
Birth of Accton
Accton was established on 
February 9, 1988 in Hsinchu 
Science Park, Taiwan.
The company began by 
designing and manufacturing 
Ethernet and networking 
products, focusing on 
network hardware and 
communications equipment.
Beyond traditional Ethernet
1998
OEM/ODM supplier
Accton provided network 
equipment to major OEMs, 
indicating early capacity for 
large-scale manufacturing 
and design flexibility.
By early 2000s, Accton’s 
revenue mix already 
included switches, wireless 
LAN, broadband/gateway 
products
Early 2000s
1995
In November 1995, Accton became 
publicly listed on the Taiwan Stock 
Exchange (stock code: 2345). 
2004 - 2010
Edgecore was founded in 2004, 
and formally spun off in 2010 
(as Accton’s subsidiary) to 
handle global branded business
Birth of Edgecore Networks
2014
Pioneering open-
network switch designs
In 2014 Accton submitted the first 
network design fully approved by 
Open Compute Project (OCP): a 10 
GbE top-of-rack switch
In 2015, Accton contributed the 
industry’s first open-design 100 
GbE Ethernet switch (and also a 
design for 40 GbE) via OCP
Late 2010s
Growing role as major white-
box / hyperscale supplier
Accton’s white-box switch business 
grew substantially; according to 
market analyses, Accton became one 
of the world’s top white-box switch 
manufacturers.
2021–2022
Transition toward 400G and 
preparation for AI / HPC 
networking demands
As demand for bandwidth surged 
(5G, Wi-Fi 6, IoT, cloud, AI), Accton 
responded by developing and 
shipping 400G switches at scale
2026
Accton continues to 
build the backbone 
that connects the 
world.
2023-2025
Push toward 800G - 1.6T, AI/ML-
optimized fabrics, open infrastructure 
& advanced system design
