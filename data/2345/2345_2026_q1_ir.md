1995

handle global branded business

2014
Pioneering open-
network switch designs

network design fully approved by 
Open Compute Project (OCP): a 10 
GbE top-of-rack switch
In 2015, Accton contributed the 
industry’s first open-design 100 
GbE Ethernet switch (and also a 
design for 40 GbE) via OCP
Late 2010s
Growing role as major white-
box / hyperscale supplier
Accton’s white-box switch business 
grew substantially; according to 
market analyses, Accton became one 
of the world’s top white-box switch 
manufacturers.
2021–2022
Transition toward 400G and 
preparation for AI / HPC 
networking demands

shipping 400G switches at scale
