# Lenovo Group Limited (HKSE: 992 / ADR: LNVGY) — FY2026/27 First Quarter Results Announcement

Source: Official HKEX-style results announcement, `https://doc.irasia.com/listco/hk/lenovo/interim/2027/int1q.pdf` (three months ended June 30, 2026, unaudited, retrieved 2026-08-31). Press release: `https://investor.lenovo.com/en/financial/results/press_2627_q1.pdf`. Investor presentation: `https://investor.lenovo.com/en/financial/results/presentation_2627_q1.pdf`.

Announced: August 13, 2026. Period: three months ended June 30, 2026 (Lenovo's official fiscal label: **Q1 FY2026/27**; comparative period: three months ended June 30, 2025).

## Financial Highlights (per official press release)

- Group revenue increased 43% YoY, reaching a record quarterly high of **US$26.9 billion**.
- **Adjusted profit attributable to equity holders** (non-HKFRS, excluding non-cash fair value loss on 2025 warrants, notional interest on convertible bonds, and other non-cash items) grew to **US$1,075 million**, up 176% YoY, surpassing the US$1.0 billion milestone for the first time. Adjusted net profit margin 4.0%.
- AI-related revenue increased 60% YoY to US$9.3 billion, 35% of Group revenue.
- All three Business Groups (IDG, ISG, SSG) delivered record first-fiscal-quarter revenue with double-digit YoY growth.
- IDG revenue US$17.1 billion (+27% YoY), operating margin stable at 7.1%. Global PC market share 24.2% (#1, gap over #2 widened for 10th consecutive quarter). Smartphone revenue record for a first fiscal quarter (+15% YoY).
- ISG revenue US$8.5 billion (+98% YoY, record), operating margin expanded to a record 9.1%, operating profit US$777 million (record; prior-year period was an operating **loss** of US$86 million). AI server pipeline US$54.0 billion, +157% QoQ.
- SSG revenue US$2.9 billion (+28% YoY, record), operating margin expanded to a record 24.2%, operating profit US$697 million (+39% YoY). Managed Services + Projects & Solutions = 62.4% of SSG revenue (record).
- R&D expense US$682 million, +30% YoY.

## ⚠️ Reported (HKFRS statutory) result: a NET LOSS, not a profit

> "Reported net loss attributable to equity holders was US$609 million, primarily due to a non-cash fair value loss of US$1.7 billion arising from the revaluation of warrants issued in 2025, and US$30 million of notional interest on convertible bonds. The warrant revaluation largely reflects changes in the Group's share price and is expected to remain a non-cash accounting item through the end of fiscal year 2027/28."

Every headline figure in press coverage of this quarter ("record profit", "$1.075B adjusted net income, +176% YoY") is the **non-HKFRS adjusted** measure. The company's own statutory HKFRS financial statements, prepared under Hong Kong Financial Reporting Standards and filed with HKEX, show a **loss** for the period.

The **US$1,690 million non-cash fair value loss on warrants** (issued in 2025, presumably in connection with a prior financing/strategic-investment transaction) is by far the single largest adjustment — it alone exceeds the entire adjusted-profit figure. Note the mirror-image effect in the prior-year quarter: Q1 FY25/26 booked a **fair value GAIN of US$152 million** on the same warrants, which is why reported profit (US$505M) was actually closer to adjusted profit (US$389M) a year ago — the current quarter's huge reported/adjusted gap is a new, quarter-specific phenomenon driven by the swing in Lenovo's own share price (the warrants are presumably struck against Lenovo stock, so a rising Lenovo share price increases the fair value of the warrant liability, which is booked as a loss).

Also note a partially offsetting item not in the bridge table above but disclosed in the narrative: fair value **gains** on strategic investments of US$250 million (2025/26 comparative not fully disclosed in the excerpt) — this is the "Net fair value changes on financial assets at FVTPL" line, which nets to a small negative in this bridge because it includes both gains and losses across positions.

## Segment Detail (from investor presentation / press release)

### Intelligent Devices Group (IDG)
- Revenue US$17.1B (+27% YoY), record for a first fiscal quarter. Operating margin 7.1% (flat YoY).
- PCSD (PC + Smart Devices adjacencies) revenue +30% YoY.
- Global PC market share 24.2% (+0.5 pts YoY), #1, lead over #2 widened for 10 consecutive quarters — the only one of the top-3 PC vendors gaining global share in Q1 while maintaining stable OPM.
- AI PC market share 25.1% (record, global #1 in AI PCs).
- Tablet revenue +83% YoY (record Q1). Smartphone revenue +15% YoY (record Q1); premium smartphone (Moto Razr/Signature/Edge) revenue mix reached a record 37%.

### Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG)
- Revenue US$8.5B (+98% YoY, record). Operating profit US$777M (record; vs an operating **loss** of US$86M a year ago). Operating margin 9.1% (record; vs −2.0% a year ago).
- CSP (Cloud Service Provider) revenue nearly doubled YoY (~2x); Enterprise & SMB (ESMB) revenue also nearly doubled YoY (~2x).
- AI server revenue grew >2.5x YoY in international markets (ex-China).
- x86 server business rose to global #2.
- AI server pipeline US$54B, +157% QoQ, driven by GB300 deployments and Vera Rubin rack-solution readiness.
- North Carolina Smart Campus expansion announced to add server-manufacturing capacity for hyperscaler/enterprise demand.

### Solutions and Services Group (SSG)
- Revenue US$2.9B (+28% YoY, record). Operating profit US$697M (+39% YoY). Operating margin 24.2% (record, +2.0 pts YoY).
- Managed Services + Projects & Solutions = 62.4% of SSG revenue (record), supported by multi-quarter booking backlog.
- TruScale (Infrastructure-as-a-Service) bookings ~2x YoY.
- AI services revenue: triple-digit YoY growth.
- Company's internal model: total SSG TAM to exceed US$850B by FY29/30, with AI-led TAM ~doubling from FY26/27 to FY29/30; SSG's share of AI-led TAM projected to rise from 37% (FY26/27) to ~50% (FY29/30).

### Geography
All regions grew YoY: PRC +25%, Asia Pacific +28%, EMEA +53%, Americas/Global (AG) +58%.

### FIFA World Cup 2026 (Official Technology Partner)
Lenovo supported all 104 matches across 3 host countries / 16 cities as Official Technology Partner: devices, infrastructure, solutions and services; AI-powered tools (FIFA AI Pro, Referee View AI Stabilizer, 3D Player Avatars). Reported reach: 6B+ fans, 99.99% solution uptime, 25K+ devices deployed, 600+ FIFA sites supported with 350+ engineers onsite, 16K guests hosted. Framed by management as a brand/customer-engagement platform rather than a direct near-term revenue driver.
