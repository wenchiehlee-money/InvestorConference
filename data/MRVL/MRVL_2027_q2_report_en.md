# MRVL FY2027 Q2 Earnings Release Summary

Source: https://investor.marvell.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1031/marvell-technology-inc-reports-second-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2027-financial-results

Date: 2026-08-27
Quarter: FY2027 Q2, quarter ended 2026-08-01

## Official Release Highlights

- Q2 net revenue was $2.739 billion, a new record, up 37% year over year.
- GAAP gross margin was 53.1%; non-GAAP gross margin was 58.9%.
- GAAP diluted income per share was $0.33; non-GAAP diluted income per share was $0.94.
- GAAP net income was $308.0 million; non-GAAP net income was $865.9 million.
- Operating cash flow was $605.5 million.
- Data center revenue growth accelerated to 46% year over year.
- Management raised revenue outlook for both FY2027 and FY2028 versus the prior quarter, citing broad strength across Data Center, Connectivity, and Custom business acceleration in the second half of FY2027.

## Q3 FY2027 Outlook

- Revenue: $3.150 billion +/- 5%.
- GAAP gross margin: 52.9% to 53.9%.
- Non-GAAP gross margin: 57.5% to 58.5%.
- GAAP operating expenses: approximately $1.015 billion.
- Non-GAAP operating expenses: approximately $655 million.
- Diluted weighted-average shares: 921 million.
- GAAP diluted EPS: $0.53 +/- $0.05.
- Non-GAAP diluted EPS: $1.10 +/- $0.05.
