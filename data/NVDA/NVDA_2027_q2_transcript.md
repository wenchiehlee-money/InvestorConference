## New Revenue-Sharing Structure with Neo Clouds

Neo clouds are seeing strong demand pipelines for many diverse offtakers. Rather than allocating their entire capacity to a single long term offtake guarantee that lenders typically require to finance a data center independently, we have introduced a revenue sharing structure. NVIDIA provides a take or pay commitment on a portion of the facility’s capacity, a minimum revenue guarantee that gives lenders the confidence to underwrite the project, and in exchange, we share in a portion of the neo cloud’s revenue earned above that floor. Independent capital still underwrites every deal on its own merits. We’re not making loans.
