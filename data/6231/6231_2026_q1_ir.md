BMC – OpenBMC Firmware  
OpenBMC is an open-source firmware project used for 
managing Baseboard Management Controllers (BMCs) in 
servers, storage, and networking devices. It enables remote 
monitoring, management, and recovery of systems 
independent of the main operating system.

Disclosure Fee
Customers pay for a set(s) 
of Insyde source code 
supporting a specific CRB* 
provided by IHV**.
* CRB: Customer Reference Board
** IHV: Independent Hardware Vendor, 
     ex. Intel, AMD,…, etc.
Royalty  Fee
Customers pay “per unit” fee 
to Insyde for their product 
shipped with Insyde’s 
firmware on it.

(Non-Recurring 
Engineering) Fee

to complete the defined tasks in the

(NRE fee per day) * (number 
of working man days defined 
in the SOW)
