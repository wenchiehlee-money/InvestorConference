Novatek Announces 2026 Second Quarter Consolidated Financial 
Result & 2026 Third Quarter Outlook: 
 
  Second Quarter Revenue & PAT Reached  
NT$28.66B & NT$6.33B Respectively  
With EPS of NT$10.40 
 
Hsin-chu, Taiwan, ROC – Aug 6th, 2026 – Novatek (TSE:3034), a leading global smart display 
and image total solution provider, today announced its second quarter consolidated financial result 
of 2026 and the third quarter outlook during the investor conference. 
 
The second quarter revenue was up by 23.83% quarter-over-quarter to NT$28.66 billion from 
NT$23.15 billion in Q1’26, and increased by 9.59% year-over-year from NT$26.15 billion in Q2’25. 
The second quarter gross margin increased by 3.36%p quarter-over-quarter to 42.42% from 
39.06% in Q1’26, and increased by 6.12%p year-over-year from 36.30% in Q2’25. Driven by 
increased demand from customers’ pull-ins during the second quarter, all of three Company's 
product lines recorded the sequential growth. As a result, both revenue and profit increased 
significantly compared with the first quarter. 
 
Second quarter operating expenses increased quarter-over-quarter to NT$5.69 billion from NT$5.01 
billion in Q1’26, and increased year-over-year from NT$4.67 billion in Q2’25. Second quarter 
operating income increased by 60.5% quarter-over-quarter to NT$6.47 billion from NT$4.03 billion 
in Q1’26, and increased by 34.1% year-over-year from NT$4.82 billion in Q2’25. 
 
Novatek’s 2026 Q2 net income increased by 67.97% quarter-over-quarter to NT$6.33 billion, and 
increased by 69.22% year-over-year from NT$3.74 billion in Q2’25.Earnings per share for Q2’26 
were NT$10.40, compared with NT$6.14 in Q2’25. 
 
 
 
Management expects overall performance for the third quarter of 2026 to be as follows: 
Revenue is expected to be between NT$30.0 billion and NT$31.2 billion 
(based on the exchange rate @1:32.0) 
Gross profit margin is expected to be between 38.5% and 41.5% 
Operating profit margin is expected to be between 19% and 22%

About Novatek 
Novatek is a trusted partner in smart imaging and smart display technologies, providing 
comprehensive and highly integrated solutions. Established in 1997, the company has grown to 
become the world’s largest supplier of display-related ICs. In 2025, Novatek ranked as the 
ninth-largest IC design company globally and the third largest in Taiwan by revenue (TW. 3034). 
For more information, please visit the company’s website at www.novatek.com.tw.
