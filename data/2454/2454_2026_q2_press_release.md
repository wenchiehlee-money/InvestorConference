MediaTek discloses Taiwan-International Financial Reporting Standards (TIFRS) financial 
measures and also provides Non-Taiwan-International Financial Reporting Standards 
(Non-TIFRS)Note1 financial measures as supplemental information. Non-TIFRS financial

Consolidated Revenue 
MediaTek Inc. today announced financial results of its second quarter ended June 30, 2026. The 
second-quarter revenue was NT$152,183 million, up 2.0% quarter-over-quarter and 1.2% year-over-year. 
The quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year increases were mainly due to market share expansion in the 
Smart Edge Platforms, which offset declining smartphone demand, highlighting the resilience of the 
diversified product portfolio. 
 
Consolidated Gross Profit and Gross Margin  
The second-quarter gross profit was NT$70,308 million, up 1.8% quarter-over-quarter and down 4.8% 
year-over-year. Gross margin for the quarter was 46.2%, down 0.1 percentage points quarter-over-quarter 
and 2.9 percentage points year-over-year. The quarter-over-quarter decrease in gross margin mainly 
reflected gross margin changes in certain products. The year-over-year decrease was mainly due to a 
one-time benefit in the year-ago quarter. 
 
Consolidated Operating Expenses 
Operating expenses for the quarter were NT$47,440 million (31.2% of revenue), which increased from 
NT$46,165 million (31.0% of revenue) in the previous quarter and NT$44,499 million (29.6% of revenue) in 
the year-ago quarter. The quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year increases were mainly due to continued 
R&D investments.

Consolidated Operating Income and Operating Margin  
Operating income for the quarter was NT$22,868 million, down 0.1% sequentially and 22.2% year-over-year. 
Operating margin for the quarter was 15.0%, down from 15.3% in the previous quarter and 19.5% in the 
year-ago quarter. 
 
Consolidated Non-operating Income (loss) and Income Tax  
Non-operating income for the quarter was NT$5,094 million, or 3.3% of revenue, mainly from interest income 
and dividend income. Income tax expense for the quarter was NT$3,357 million. 
 
Consolidated Net Income, Net Profit Margin and EPS  
Net income for the quarter was NT$24,605 million, up 0.9% sequentially and down 12.3% year-over-year. 
Net profit margin for the quarter was 16.2%, down from 16.3% in the previous quarter and 18.7% in the 
year-ago quarter. EPS was NT$15.28, up from NT$15.17 in the previous quarter and down from NT$17.50 in 
the year-ago quarter. The quarter-over-quarter increases in net income and EPS were mainly due to higher 
revenue in the quarter. The year-over-year decreases in net income, net profit margin and EPS were mainly 
due to lower gross margin in the quarter. 
 
Consolidated Cash and Financial Assets-Current  
Cash and financial assets-current at the end of the quarter was NT$198,403 million. This represents 25.2% of 
the company’s total assets. Cash and financial assets-current was NT$194,779 million and NT$196,735 million 
at the end of previous quarter and the year-ago quarter, respectively. Financial assets-current portfolio 
includes mutual funds, bonds, derivatives and so on. 
 
Consolidated Accounts Receivable 
Accounts receivable (net) at the end of the quarter was NT$76,981 million. The accounts receivable turnover 
was 47 days based on quarterly average net receivables divided by annualized net revenue. The turnover was 
higher than 43 days in the previous quarter and 45 days the year-ago quarter. 
 
Consolidated Inventory 
Net inventory at the end of the quarter was NT$98,421 million. The inventory turnover was 102 days based on 
quarterly average inventory divided by annualized cost of goods sold. The turnover was higher than 87 days in 
the previous quarter and 66 days in the year-ago quarter. 
 
Consolidated Cash Flow from Operations  
Net cash provided by operating activities during the quarter was NT$24,251 million, compared with net cash 
used in operating activities of NT$17,657 million in the previous quarter and net cash provided by operating 
activities of NT$45,669 million in the year-ago quarter.
