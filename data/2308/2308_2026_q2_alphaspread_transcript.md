Delta Electronics Inc
Investor Relations

Delta Electronics makes power and thermal management products that help devices and factories use electricity efficiently. Its core products include power supplies, industrial automation equipment, cooling systems, and charging hardware for electric vehicles. The company also sells electronics parts used inside computers, telecom gear, and industrial machines. Its customers are mainly other businesses and institutions, not everyday consumers. Delta sells to makers of electronics, factory operators, telecom companies, and transportation and energy customers that need reliable power conversion, control, and cooling. It earns money by designing, manufacturing, and selling these hardware systems and components, often as part of long-term supply relationships. What makes Delta different is its role at the center of the electrical chain: it does not usually make the finished consumer product, but it supplies the power and thermal technology that helps those products and systems run safely and efficiently. That gives it a broad position across electronics, industrial automation, and electrification markets.
